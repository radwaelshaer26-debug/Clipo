# كل سطر = كلمة | المعنى بالعربي | جملة مثال
# السطور اللي بتبدأ بعلامة # بيتجاهلها التطبيق
persevere | يثابر | She persevered despite the difficulties.
reluctant | متردد، غير راغب | He was reluctant to speak in class.
ambitious | طموح | She is an ambitious student.
curious | فضولي | Children are naturally curious.
achieve | يحقق | You can achieve your goals with hard work.
improve | يحسّن | I want to improve my English.
confident | واثق من نفسه | Be confident when you speak.
determined | مصمم | He is determined to pass the exam.
essential | أساسي | Sleep is essential for good health.
benefit | فائدة | Reading has many benefits.
challenge | تحدٍّ | Learning a language is a challenge.
opportunity | فرصة | This is a great opportunity to learn.
accurate | دقيق | Make sure your answer is accurate.
fluent | متمكّن في اللغة | She is fluent in three languages.
vocabulary | مفردات | Reading helps you build your vocabulary.
pronounce | ينطق | How do you pronounce this word?
summarize | يلخّص | Summarize the story in three sentences.
compare | يقارن | Compare these two answers.
focus | يركّز | Turn off your phone and focus.
deadline | موعد نهائي | The deadline for the project is Friday.
require | يتطلب | This job requires patience.
recommend | ينصح بـ | I recommend this book.
consider | يفكر في | Consider all the options first.
attempt | محاولة | It was his first attempt.
exhausted | مرهق جدًا | I was exhausted after the exam.
delighted | سعيد جدًا | We are delighted to see you.
ordinary | عادي | It was an ordinary day.
rarely | نادرًا | He rarely arrives late.
gradually | تدريجيًا | Her English improved gradually.
eventually | في النهاية | Eventually, she passed the test.
